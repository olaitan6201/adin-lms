# Privacy case procedure

**Entry points:** `/operations/consent` (subject); `/operations` (ICT retention setting); `/operations/privacy` (authorised privacy operators). Use named accounts with MFA for staff. An operator cannot approve or execute their own subject request.

1. Verify the requester, legal authority and exact account UUID using the institution's approved channel. Check records holds and current retention policy with the privacy lead. Keep documents and free-text case notes in the restricted case system, not in audit references.
2. For optional feedback, the subject can grant or withdraw notice version 1. No consent is implied by using a course. Repeating the same choice creates no extra active record. Withdrawal dates the consent instead of removing the evidence immediately.
3. ICT configures **only** the retention period for withdrawn feedback consents (1–3650 days). Without a setting purge is disabled. Enter the institution's approved value, not a trial default. Adjusting the period alone does not erase anything.
4. On the privacy desk enter the subject UUID and an opaque case reference (letters, digits, `_` or `-`, maximum 64 characters). Select **Export account and consent** or **Delete eligible withdrawn feedback consents**. Save the returned approval UUID to the restricted case.
5. A different authorised operator checks the subject, original case, action, holds and retention setting, then enters the subject and approval UUID. Approval is single-use, subject-bound and expires after 24 hours. Purge eligibility uses the approval date; a changed retention period requires fresh approval. The application does not adjudicate legal holds; do not approve or execute while a hold exists.
6. Export JSON contains only the subject's account ID, full name, email, status and their consent records. Deliver using the approved encrypted channel, verify the recipient, record delivery and securely dispose of the working copy. Credentials, sessions, other accounts, assessment answers and grades are excluded. Broader access requests need separate reviewed extracts; this is not a full subject access response.
7. Purge returns the number of expired, withdrawn optional-feedback rows removed for that subject. Active consents, other purposes, registrations, grades and audit history remain. Never interpret this as general account erasure. Audit actions include `retention.changed`, `privacy.approved`, `privacy.executed`, `feedback.granted` and `feedback.withdrawn`. Approval links the executor receipt to the case and subject.

**Incident drill:** Use a synthetic report of a misdirected export. Stop further delivery, revoke access to the shared copy where possible, preserve minimal evidence and identify affected subjects/recipients. Immediately contact the institution's privacy/security lead through the agreed incident channel; record the external incident reference with **Record referral**. This produces an audit receipt, not a notification. The designated lead assesses notification obligations and deadlines under applicable law (including Nigeria's NDPA), coordinates containment, and records decisions, communications and remediation. Do not use real subjects for training.
